Debrief — three decision points students most often miss

Treating on the screen.

The 1-hour test is a screen; diagnosis requires the 3-hour OGTT.

Jumping to insulin.

First-line is nutrition, activity, and monitoring; insulin is reserved for missed targets.

Over-restricting carbohydrates.

Balanced, distributed carbohydrate intake — not elimination or fasting — is the goal.
