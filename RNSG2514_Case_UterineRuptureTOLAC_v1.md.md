6 scenes, one per NCJMM operation.

Camila, 32, G2 P1, at 40 weeks, attempting a trial of labor after one prior low-transverse cesarean (TOLAC). Oxytocin augmentation running.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Scene 1 — Recognize Cues (highlight)

Sudden severe, tearing abdominal pain reported between contractions; the fetal station, previously 0, is now higher; FHR drops to the 60s–70s (prolonged bradycardia); maternal HR rising, BP falling.

Highlight the cues of uterine rupture.

acute tearing pain + loss of station (rising presenting part) + prolonged fetal bradycardia during TOLAC.

This triad during TOLAC is uterine rupture until proven otherwise \[ACOG PB 205\].

Rupture is a dual emergency — fetal hypoxia and maternal bleeding — demanding immediate surgery \[ACOG PB 205\].

Scene 4 — Generate Solutions (extended multiple response)

Select the 3 correct immediate actions.

✅ Stop the oxytocin infusion

✅ Call for help and prepare for emergency cesarean

✅ Begin maternal resuscitation measures (IV fluids, prepare for transfusion)

❌ Increase oxytocin to deliver faster

❌ Continue expectant management

❌ Delay surgery to obtain a formal ultrasound first

Stop stimulation, mobilize surgery, and resuscitate; delay costs the fetus and mother \[ACOG PB 205\].

Scene 5 — Take Actions (SATA)

Which nursing actions apply now? Select all that apply.

✅ Discontinue oxytocin immediately

✅ Notify the surgical and neonatal teams

✅ Establish large-bore IV access and prepare blood products

✅ Position for optimal perfusion and give oxygen if maternal hypoxia is present

❌ Administer a uterotonic to augment contractions

Every action supports the dual emergency; augmenting contractions is contraindicated \[ACOG PB 205\].

Scene 6 — Evaluate Outcomes (trend)

minute 0 FHR 65, maternal HR 118, BP 92/54; minute 10 in OR, delivery accomplished; post-op maternal HR 96, BP 110/68 after fluids/blood; neonate resuscitated, Apgar 4→8.

Which interpretation best evaluates the response?

Rapid recognition and emergency cesarean with resuscitation stabilized mother and infant — the escalation was timely and effective.

Evaluate by time-to-delivery, maternal hemodynamic recovery, and neonatal response \[ACOG PB 205\].
