## SLIDE 04 — BLEEDING: EARLY vs LATE
**Image:** `2514_D1_S04_bleeding.jpg`
```
BLEEDING IN PREGNANCY

| Timing | Causes |
|---|---|
| EARLY (<20 wk) | Abortion, ectopic, GTD, cervical insufficiency |
| LATE (>20 wk) | Previa, abruption, accreta |

Bleeding = potentially life-threatening.
Recognition → assessment → resuscitation.
```

## SLIDE 05 — SPONTANEOUS ABORTION
**Image:** `2514_D1_S05_abortion.jpg`
```
SPONTANEOUS ABORTION (MISCARRIAGE)

Loss before 20 weeks, natural causes.
Most common complication (up to 31%).

Categories: threatened, inevitable,
incomplete, complete, missed, septic.

Loss after 20 weeks = stillbirth.
```

---

## SLIDE 06 — ABORTION CATEGORIES
**Image:** `2514_D1_S06_abortion-types.jpg`
```
SPONTANEOUS ABORTION TYPES

| Type | Bleeding | Cervix |
|---|---|---|
| Threatened | Present | Closed |
| Inevitable | Present | Dilated |
| Incomplete | Present + tissue | Dilated |
| Complete | Decreased | Closed |
| Missed | Absent/brown | Closed |
| Septic | Present + fever | Dilated |
```

## SLIDE 07 — ABORTION NURSING PRIORITIES
**Image:** `2514_D1_S07_abortion-nursing.jpg`
```
ABORTION NURSING PRIORITIES

→ Assess color, amount, clots, tissue.
→ Save any tissue passed.
→ Monitor VS, watch for shock.
→ Prepare suction curettage / D&C.
→ RhoGAM within 72h if Rh-negative.
→ Reassure: not the parent's fault.
```

## SLIDE 08 — ECTOPIC PREGNANCY
**Image:** `2514_D1_S08_ectopic.png`
```
ECTOPIC PREGNANCY

Fertilized ovum implants outside uterus
(96% fallopian tube).

Risk: prior ectopic, PID, IUD, smoking.

Treatment:
→ Stable: IM methotrexate
→ Ruptured: surgery (salpingectomy)
```

## SLIDE 09 — TAKE NOTE: ECTOPIC RUPTURE
**Image:** `2514_D1_S09_ectopic-rupture.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! ECTOPIC RUPTURE

Hallmark: abdominal pain with spotting
6–8 weeks after missed period.

Shoulder pain = referred intra-abdominal
blood → suggests RUPTURE.

Surgical emergency — hypotension,
tachycardia = shock.
```

---

## SLIDE 10 — GTD (MOLAR PREGNANCY)
**Image:** `2514_D1_S10_molar.png`
```
GESTATIONAL TROPHOBLASTIC DISEASE

Abnormal trophoblast overgrowth.
Pregnancy not viable. hCG = marker.

Hydatidiform mole: grape-like vesicles.
Complete = empty egg + sperm.
Partial = triploid (69).

Findings: bleeding, uterus > dates,
hyperemesis, preeclampsia <20 wk.
```

---

## SLIDE 11 — GTD MANAGEMENT
**Image:** `2514_D1_S11_molar-mgmt.jpg`
```
GTD MANAGEMENT

→ Evacuate by D&C (or hysterectomy).
→ Serial beta-hCG until non-detectable.
→ Reliable contraception 6–12 months.
→ Methotrexate chemo if indicated.

Pregnancy contraindicated 6–12 months
(hides rising hCG).
```

---

## SLIDE 12 — CERVICAL INSUFFICIENCY
**Image:** `2514_D1_S12_cervical.png`
```
CERVICAL INSUFFICIENCY

Painless cervical dilation in 2nd
trimester → expulsion of immature fetus.

Risk: prior trauma, 2nd-tri loss, DES.

Findings: pink discharge, pelvic
pressure, painless dilation.

Management: cerclage (purse-string).
```

## SLIDE 13 — PREVIA vs ABRUPTION
**Image:** `2514_D1_S13_previa-abruption.png`
```
PREVIA vs ABRUPTION

| Feature | Previa | Abruption |
|---|---|---|
| Bleeding | Painless, bright red | Painful, dark |
| Uterine tone | Soft | Rigid, tender |
| FHR | Usually normal | Distress/absent |
| Onset | Insidious | Sudden |
```

---

## SLIDE 14 — PLACENTA PREVIA KEY POINTS
**Image:** `2514_D1_S14_previa.jpg`
```
PLACENTA PREVIA

Placenta covers internal cervical os.
Risk: prior cesarean, multiparity.

NEVER perform vaginal exam
(disrupts placenta → hemorrhage).

Care: IV, type & crossmatch,
fetal monitoring, cesarean birth.
```

---

## SLIDE 15 — PLACENTAL ABRUPTION KEY POINTS
**Image:** `2514_D1_S15_abruption.png`
```
PLACENTAL ABRUPTION

Premature separation after 20 weeks.
Risk: hypertension, trauma, cocaine.

Emergency: 2 large-bore IVs,
type & crossmatch, O2, fetal monitoring.

Complication: DIC, fetal death.
Watch: bleeding gums, oozing IV site.
```

---

## SLIDE 16 — TAKE NOTE: BLOOD LOSS
**Image:** `2514_D1_S16_bloodloss.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! OCCULT BLOOD LOSS

Vital signs look NORMAL even with
30–40% blood loss in pregnancy.

Expanded blood volume masks shock.

Do NOT wait for hypotension
to recognize hemorrhage.
```

---

## SLIDE 17 — DIC
**Image:** `2514_D1_S17_dic.png`
```
DIC — DISSEMINATED INTRAVASCULAR COAGULATION

Widespread clotting uses up factors,
then bleeding occurs.

Triggers: abruption, AFE, dead fetus,
sepsis, molar, HELLP.

Labs: ↓ platelets, ↓ fibrinogen,
↑ PT/aPTT, positive D-dimer.
```

## SLIDE 19 — HYPEREMESIS GRAVIDARUM
**Image:** `2514_D1_S19_hyperemesis.png`
```
HYPEREMESIS GRAVIDARUM

Severe N/V ~5–6 weeks.
Weight loss >5%, dehydration,
ketosis, ketonuria.

Why: elevated hCG, relaxed LES.

Risk: molar, multiples, family history.
```

---

## SLIDE 20 — HYPEREMESIS MANAGEMENT
**Image:** `2514_D1_S20_hyperemesis-mgmt.jpg`
```
HYPEREMESIS MANAGEMENT

1. NPO — rest the gut first.
2. IV fluids + B6 + electrolytes.
3. Antiemetics (ondansetron, Diclégis).
4. Gradual oral reintroduction.
5. TPN or PEG if no improvement.
```

---

## SLIDE 21 — TAKE NOTE: NPO FIRST
**Image:** `2514_D1_S21_npo.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! HYPEREMESIS PRIORITY

Priority = NPO until vomiting stops,
THEN rehydrate.

Do NOT force fluids by mouth —
it prolongs vomiting.
```

## SLIDE 22 — PPH: THE 4 Ts
**Image:** `2514_D1_S22_pph.png`
```
POSTPARTUM HEMORRHAGE — 4 Ts

| T | Cause | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Atony (most) | Massage + oxytocin |
| Trauma | Lacerations | Inspect, repair |
| Tissue | Retained placenta | Evacuate |
| Thrombin | Coagulopathy | Clotting factors |

## SLIDE 36 — QUICK RECALL (1/3)
**Image:** `2514_D1_S36_recall1.jpg`
```
QUICK RECALL — PART 1

Spontaneous abortion: loss <20 wk,
save tissue, RhoGAM if Rh−.

Ectopic: pain + spotting 6–8 wk,
shoulder pain = rupture.

Molar: uterus > dates, track hCG,
no pregnancy 6–12 months.

Cervical insufficiency: painless
dilation, cerclage.
```

---

## SLIDE 37 — QUICK RECALL (2/3)
**Image:** `2514_D1_S37_recall2.jpg`
```
QUICK RECALL — PART 2

Previa: painless bright red, soft uterus,
NO vaginal exam.

Abruption: painful dark, rigid uterus,
fetal distress, watch for DIC.

DIC: ↓ platelets, ↓ fibrinogen,
↑ PT/aPTT, + D-dimer.

Hyperemesis: N/V + >5% loss,
NPO first, then IV fluids.
```

---

## SLIDE 38 — QUICK RECALL (3/3)
**Image:** `2514_D1_S38_recall3.jpg`
```
QUICK RECALL — PART 3

PPH 4 Ts: Tone, Trauma, Tissue, Thrombin.
Uterine atony = most common.

Methylergonovine: ✗ hypertension.
Carboprost: ✗ asthma.
TXA: within 3 hours.

QBL beats visual estimation.
Vital signs can hide 30–40% loss.
