## SLIDE 06 — MAGNESIUM SULFATE
**Image:** `2514_D2_S06_magnesium.png`
```
MAGNESIUM SULFATE

Seizure prophylaxis — NOT antihypertensive.
Severe BP needs separate antihypertensive.

Dose: 4–6 g load, then 1–2 g/hour.
Monitor: DTRs, RR, urine output.

Toxicity: RR <12, absent DTRs,
UO <30 mL/h.
```

---

## SLIDE 07 — TAKE NOTE: MAG TOXICITY
**Image:** `2514_D2_S07_mag-toxicity.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! MAGNESIUM TOXICITY

Sequence: loss of DTRs →
respiratory depression → cardiac arrest.

DTRs disappear FIRST.

Antidote: calcium gluconate
— keep at bedside.

First action: STOP infusion,
give calcium gluconate.
```

## SLIDE 08 — HELLP SYNDROME
**Image:** `2514_D2_S08_hellp.jpg`
```
HELLP SYNDROME

Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes,
Low Platelets.

Severe preeclampsia variant (4–12%).
Findings: RUQ pain, nausea, headache.

Labs: ↓ Hgb, ↑ LDH, ↑ AST/ALT,
↓ platelets <100,000.
```

---

## SLIDE 09 — GDM OVERVIEW
**Image:** `2514_D2_S09_gdm.png`
```
GESTATIONAL DIABETES MELLITUS

Glucose intolerance onset in pregnancy.
Placental hormones → insulin resistance.

Risk: obesity, prior GDM, PCOS,
family history, prior LGA infant.

A1 = diet-controlled. A2 = medication.
```

## SLIDE 10 — GDM SCREENING & TARGETS
**Image:** `2514_D2_S10_gdm-screening.jpg`
```
GDM SCREENING (75g OGTT, 24–28 wk)

| Measure | Diagnostic | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting | ≥92 | <95 |
| 1-hour | ≥180 | <140 |
| 2-hour | ≥153 | <120 |

Insulin = first-line medication.
```

## SLIDE 12 — PRETERM LABOR
**Image:** `2514_D2_S12_preterm.png`
```
PRETERM LABOR

Regular contractions + cervical change
before 37 weeks.

Warning: discharge change, pelvic
pressure, backache, cramps, >6 ctx/hour.

Testing: cervical length <30 mm,
fFN >50 ng/mL.
```

## SLIDE 14 — PROM & PPROM
**Image:** `2514_D2_S14_prom.jpg`

PROM & PPROM

PROM = rupture at ≥37 weeks.

(higher infection risk).

→ Pooling, fern test, nitrazine blue.
```

NO digital cervical exam —

## SLIDE 30 — QUICK RECALL (1/2)
**Image:** `2514_D2_S30_recall1.jpg`
```
QUICK RECALL — PART 1

Chronic HTN: ≥140/90 before 20 wk.
Gestational HTN: after 20 wk, no protein.
Preeclampsia: HTN + proteinuria after 20.
Eclampsia: preeclampsia + seizures.

HELLP: Hemolysis, Liver, Low Platelets.
DIC: ↓ platelets, ↓ fibrinogen, +D-dimer.

Magnesium: seizure prophylaxis, antidote
= calcium gluconate.
```

## SLIDE 31 — QUICK RECALL (2/2)
**Image:** `2514_D2_S31_recall2.jpg`
```
QUICK RECALL — PART 2

GDM: OGTT 24–28 wk, fasting target <95,
insulin first-line.

Preterm labor: contractions + change,
betamethasone + tocolytics.

PROM/PPROM: no digital exam,
nitrazine/ferning, watch infection.

Chorioamnionitis: fever, tachycardia,
tender uterus → deliver.
