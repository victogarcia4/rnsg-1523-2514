## SLIDE 04 — SCREENING vs DIAGNOSTIC
**Image:** `1523_D4_S04_screening.jpg`
```
SCREENING vs DIAGNOSTIC

Screening = identifies RISK.
Diagnostic = CONFIRMS diagnosis.

| Feature | Screen | Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Risk | Confirm |
| Method | Noninvasive | May be invasive |
| Risk | Minimal | Procedure risk |
| Result | Needs follow-up | Definitive |
```

---

## SLIDE 05 — TAKE NOTE: SCREENING NOT DIAGNOSIS
**Image:** `1523_D4_S05_screen-note.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! CRITICAL DISTINCTION

A positive SCREEN does NOT diagnose.
It estimates increased risk.

Never treat a screening result
as a diagnosis.

Positive screen → offer diagnostic
testing (CVS / amniocentesis).
```

## SLIDE 08 — MSAFP & QUAD SCREEN
**Image:** `1523_D4_S08_msafp.jpg`
```
MSAFP & QUAD SCREEN

MSAFP 15–20 wk (optimal 16–18):
→ ↑ = NTD, omphalocele, multiples
→ ↓ = trisomy 21/18

Quad = AFP + hCG + uE3 + inhibin A.
Improves Down syndrome detection.

Abnormal → ultrasound + counseling.
```

## SLIDE 11 — ULTRASOUND
**Image:** `1523_D4_S11_ultrasound.png`
```
ULTRASOUND

Noninvasive, no known risks.
1st tri transvaginal = most accurate dating.
18–20 wk: anatomy scan.
32–36 wk: growth/position.

Visualizes: GA, fetal number, viability,
anatomy, placenta, amniotic fluid.
```

## SLIDE 12 — AMNIOCENTESIS
**Image:** `1523_D4_S12_amnio.png`
```
AMNIOCENTESIS (15–20 wk)

Transabdominal needle aspirates fluid.
Detects: chromosomal (>40 types), NTDs,
metabolic errors, lung maturity.

Risks: miscarriage ~1/300–500, infection,
Rh sensitization.

Nursing: consent, RhoGAM if Rh−,
VS + FHR after, teach danger signs.
```

---

## SLIDE 13 — CVS
**Image:** `1523_D4_S13_cvs.png`
```
CVS — CHORIONIC VILLUS SAMPLING
(10–13 wk)

Aspirates chorionic villi (placental).
Earlier than amniocentesis.
Detects: chromosomal, CF, enzyme,
sex-linked disorders.

Does NOT detect NTDs (no AFP).
Risks: miscarriage ~1/200–400, bleeding.
```

---

## SLIDE 14 — NST INTERPRETATION
**Image:** `1523_D4_S14_nst.png`
```
NST — NONSTRESS TEST

REACTIVE (reassuring):
≥2 accelerations (≥15 bpm × 15 sec)
in 20 minutes.

NONREACTIVE:
Fails criteria in 40 min.
>50% false positive (sleep, prematurity).

→ Repeat, vibroacoustic, or BPP.
```

---

## SLIDE 15 — BPP SCORING
**Image:** `1523_D4_S15_bpp.png`
```
BPP — BIOPHYSICAL PROFILE

5 components (2 pts each):
→ Fetal breathing
→ Fetal movement
→ Fetal tone
→ Amniotic fluid volume
→ NST reactivity

8–10 = normal; 6 = equivocal;
≤4 = abnormal → deliver.
```

---

## SLIDE 16 — TAKE NOTE: HYPOXIA ORDER
**Image:** `1523_D4_S16_hypoxia.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! FETAL HYPOXIA ORDER

Fetus loses functions in REVERSE
developmental order:

Tone FIRST (acquired 8 wk),
then movement (9 wk),
then breathing (20 wk),
then FHR reactivity (24 wk).

Tone is the FIRST to go in hypoxia.
```

## SLIDE 18 — TAKE NOTE: DECISION RESTS WITH PATIENT
**Image:** `1523_D4_S18_decision.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! PATIENT DECIDES

Decision to test, and what to do
with results, rests with the patient.

Nurses = nondirective support.

Respect the decision.

GINA 2008: insurers/employers only,
NOT life/disability/LTC insurance.
```
