## SLIDE 07 — FOOD SAFETY
**Image:** `1523_D3_S07_food-safety.png`
```
FOOD SAFETY DURING PREGNANCY

Avoid high-mercury fish: shark, swordfish,
king mackerel, tilefish.

Listeria: no unpasteurized dairy,
deli meats (unless steaming), soft cheeses.

No alcohol — no safe amount or trimester.
Limit caffeine <200–300 mg/day.
```

## SLIDE 08 — SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
**Image:** `1523_D3_S08_pica.jpg`
```
SPECIAL NUTRITIONAL CONSIDERATIONS

Pica: ingestion of nonfood items (dirt,
ice, starch). Assess non-judgmentally.
Screen for iron-deficiency anemia.

Lactose intolerance: lactose-free dairy,
calcium-enriched substitutes.

Vegetarian/vegan: ensure protein, iron,
calcium, B12, vitamin D.
```

## SLIDE 09 — TAKE NOTE: DIETING
**Image:** `1523_D3_S09_dieting.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! NO DIETING

Dieting is NEVER recommended in pregnancy,
even with overweight or obesity.

Severe caloric restriction →
decreased birth weight.

Focus on nutrient density.
+340 cal/day in 2nd tri.
+450 cal/day in 3rd tri.
```

## SLIDE 13 — NAUSEA MANAGEMENT
**Image:** `1523_D3_S13_nausea.png`
```
NAUSEA & VOMITING MANAGEMENT

Crackers/toast before arising.
Small frequent meals (5–6/day).
Separate fluids from solids.
Avoid triggers (odors, greasy).

Ginger 1 g/day; B6 10–25 mg q6–8h.
Diclégis (doxylamine + pyridoxine).
Severe = hyperemesis gravidarum.
```

---

## SLIDE 14 — TAKE NOTE: HYPEREMESIS
**Image:** `1523_D3_S14_hyperemesis.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! HYPEREMESIS

N/V affects ~90% — but hyperemesis
is NOT normal morning sickness.

>5% weight loss + dehydration +
ketonuria = hyperemesis.

Treatment: NPO → IV fluids → antiemetics.
```

NORMAL DISCOMFORT vs WARNING SIGN

## SLIDE 17 — EDEMA: NORMAL vs DANGER
**Image:** `1523_D3_S17_edema.png`
```
EDEMA — KEY DISTINCTION

NORMAL: dependent lower-extremity edema,
improves with elevation, no ↑ BP.

DANGER: sudden facial/hand edema,
sudden weight gain, ↑ BP, proteinuria
→ PREECLAMPSIA.

Sudden facial/hand swelling = warning.
Evening ankle swelling = normal.
```

## SLIDE 18 — MEDICATIONS: PLLR
**Image:** `1523_D3_S18_pllr.jpg`
```
MEDICATIONS — PLLR SYSTEM

2015: FDA replaced A-B-C-D-X letters
with Pregnancy & Lactation Labeling Rule.

PLLR = narrative risk labeling.
Old letters still in older texts —
teach both.

ACE inhibitors/ARBs = fetotoxic.
Isotretinoin = contraindicated.
```

## SLIDE 20 — NSAIDs IN PREGNANCY
**Image:** `1523_D3_S20_nsaids.jpg`
```
NSAIDs (IBUPROFEN) — CONTRAINDICATED 3RD TRI

3rd trimester:
→ Premature ductus arteriosus closure
→ Oligohydramnios
→ Prolonged labor

Use acetaminophen instead.
1st/2nd tri: only if clearly needed.
```

IMMUNIZATIONS IN PREGNANCY

## SLIDE 22 — TAKE NOTE: Tdap
**Image:** `1523_D3_S22_tdap.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! Tdap EVERY PREGNANCY

Tdap at 27–36 weeks EACH pregnancy,
regardless of prior history.

Purpose: passive pertussis immunity
to newborn before infant vaccines.

Flu = inactivated only.
Live vaccines = contraindicated.
```

## SLIDE 24 — BREAST CARE
**Image:** `1523_D3_S24_breast-care.png`
```
BREAST CARE DURING PREGNANCY

Supportive, well-fitting bra.
Wash with water only — no soap on nipples.
Colostrum by 3rd tri — normal.
Assess flat/inverted nipples at 36 weeks.
Montgomery tubercles = normal.
Monthly breast self-exam continues.
```

## SLIDE 25 — SELF-CARE & EXERCISE
**Image:** `1523_D3_S25_exercise.png`
```
SELF-CARE & HEALTH PROMOTION

Exercise: 150 min/week moderate activity.
Stop if bleeding or fluid leakage.

Sleep: left lateral, avoid supine >20 wk.

Hygiene: no douching or tampons.

Travel: safest 2nd trimester (14–28 wk).
```

PSYCHOSOCIAL ADAPTATIONS

~15–20% experience perinatal mood disorders.
Screen at EVERY prenatal visit.

## SLIDE 28 — TAKE NOTE: PERINATAL DEPRESSION
**Image:** `1523_D3_S28_depression.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! PERINATAL DEPRESSION

Most common complication of pregnancy.

Screen with Edinburgh Scale
at every prenatal visit.

Red flags: anhedonia, persistent sadness,
thoughts of self-harm.

Sertraline = preferred SSRI.
Do NOT abruptly stop antidepressants.
```
