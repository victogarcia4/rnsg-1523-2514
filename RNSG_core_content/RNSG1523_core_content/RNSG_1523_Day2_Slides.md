## SLIDE 05 — PREEMBRYONIC STAGE
**Image:** `1523_D2_S05_preembryonic.png`
```
PREEMBRYONIC STAGE
Fertilization → end of 2nd week

Fertilization: outer third of fallopian tube.
Zygote → morula (16 cells, 72h) → blastocyst.

Implantation: 7–10 days after conception,
upper uterus (fundus).

Three germ layers:
→ Ectoderm → CNS, skin, glands
→ Mesoderm → skeletal, circulatory
→ Endoderm → respiratory, liver, GI
```

## SLIDE 06 — EMBRYONIC STAGE
**Image:** `1523_D2_S06_embryonic.png`
```
EMBRYONIC STAGE
Day 15 → end of week 8

MOST CRITICAL period — organogenesis.
All major organs and structures form.

Embryo most susceptible to teratogens
(days 17–56 after conception).

Heart begins beating ~22 days.
All organ systems present by week 8.
```

## SLIDE 07 — TAKE NOTE: TERATOGENS
**Image:** `1523_D2_S07_teratogens.png`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! TERATOGENIC RISK

Greatest risk: days 17–56 (organogenesis).

Teratogens:
→ Ionizing radiation, alcohol
→ Isotretinoin, valproic acid, warfarin
→ Rubella, CMV, toxoplasmosis, syphilis
→ Uncontrolled diabetes, lead, mercury

Placenta = pass-through, NOT a barrier.
```

## SLIDE 08 — FETAL STAGE
**Image:** `1523_D2_S08_fetal-stage.png`
```
FETAL STAGE
End of week 8 → birth

Longest stage: growth and refinement.

FHR: 110–160 bpm.
Quickening felt 16–22 weeks.
Viability ~22–24 weeks with care.

Three shunts bypass lungs/liver:
→ Ductus venosus (liver bypass)
→ Ductus arteriosus (lung bypass)
→ Foramen ovale (RA → LA)
```

```
SIGNS OF PREGNANCY

Three categories:
```

## SLIDE 10 — PRESUMPTIVE SIGNS
**Image:** `1523_D2_S10_presumptive.png`
```
PRESUMPTIVE SIGNS (Subjective)

Perceived by the patient:
→ Amenorrhea (most obvious)
→ Nausea/vomiting (~90%)
→ Breast tenderness
→ Urinary frequency (no dysuria)
→ Fatigue
→ Quickening (~18–20 wk)

NOT diagnostic — many other causes.
```

---

## SLIDE 11 — PROBABLE SIGNS
**Image:** `1523_D2_S11_probable.png`
```
PROBABLE SIGNS (Objective)

Detected by examiner:
→ Hegar sign (lower segment, ~6 wk)
→ Goodell sign (cervix soft, ~5 wk)
→ Chadwick sign (bluish, ~6–8 wk)
→ Ballottement, Braxton Hicks
→ Positive pregnancy test (hCG)

Strongly suggestive, NOT 100%.
```

---

## SLIDE 12 — POSITIVE SIGNS
**Image:** `1523_D2_S12_positive.png`
```
POSITIVE SIGNS (Diagnostic)

Directly attributable to fetus:
→ Ultrasound visualization of embryo
→ Fetal heart tones (Doppler ~10–12 wk)
→ Fetal movement felt by examiner
→ FHR on ultrasound (~5–6 wk)

100% confirms pregnancy.
```

## SLIDE 13 — hCG INTERPRETATION
**Image:** `1523_D2_S13_hcg.png`
```
hCG — HUMAN CHORIONIC GONADOTROPIN

Made by trophoblast.
<5 mIU/mL  = negative
>25 mIU/mL = positive

Doubles every 29–53 hours.
Peaks 8–10 weeks (morning sickness).
```

## SLIDE 14 — GTPAL OVERVIEW
**Image:** `1523_D2_S14_gtpal.png`
```
GTPAL — OBSTETRIC HISTORY

| Letter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| G | Gravida — total pregnancies (incl. current) |
| T | Term births (38–42 wk) |
| P | Preterm births (20–37 wk) |
| A | Abortions (before 20 wk) |
| L | Living children currently alive |
```

---

## SLIDE 15 — GTPAL COUNTING RULES
**Image:** `1523_D2_S15_gtpal-rules.jpg`
```
GTPAL COUNTING RULES

Gravida = every pregnancy + current.
Term/Preterm = birth EVENTS, not infants.
Abortions = losses before 20 weeks.
Living = each child individually.

Multiples = ONE event for T/P/A.
Multiples = TWO+ for Living.
T + P + A = G − 1
```

## SLIDE 19 — TAKE NOTE: GTPAL
**Image:** `1523_D2_S19_gtpal-note.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! GTPAL

Always include CURRENT pregnancy in Gravida.

Deceased child: subtract from L,
NOT from T, P, or A.

Multiples: one birth event for T/P/A,
individual children for L.

GTPAL > gravida/para (more detail).
```

## SLIDE 20 — NAGELE'S RULE
**Image:** `1523_D2_S20_naegele.png`
```
EDD — NAGELE'S RULE

Step 1: First day of LMP.
Step 2: Subtract 3 months.
Step 3: Add 7 days.
Step 4: Add 1 year.

Margin of error: ±2 weeks.
```

## SLIDE 22 — TAKE NOTE: OVULATION RULE
**Image:** `1523_D2_S22_ovulation-note.png`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! OVULATION TIMING

Ovulation = 14 days before NEXT menses,
regardless of cycle length.

35-day cycle → ovulation day 21.
21-day cycle → ovulation day 7.

Naegele's rule assumes 28-day cycle.
Irregular cycles → use ULTRASOUND.
```

## SLIDE 28 — MATERNAL ADAPTATIONS: CARDIOVASCULAR
**Image:** `1523_D2_S28_cardiovascular.png`
```
CARDIOVASCULAR ADAPTATIONS

Blood volume ↑ 30–50%, peaks 30–34 wk.
Plasma ↑ > RBC → physiologic anemia.

Cardiac output ↑ 30–40% (peaks 20–24 wk).
HR ↑ 10–15 bpm.
BP ↓ mid-pregnancy (progesterone).
Hypercoagulable → ↑ DVT risk.
```

```
RESPIRATORY & GI ADAPTATIONS

Respiratory:
→ Tidal volume ↑ 30–40%
```

## SLIDE 31 — TAKE NOTE: SUPINE HYPOTENSION
**Image:** `1523_D2_S31_supine.jpg`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! SUPINE HYPOTENSION

Gravid uterus compresses IVC when supine
(after 20 weeks).

Symptoms: dizziness, nausea, tachycardia,
hypotension, syncope.

Action: position LEFT LATERAL
or wedge under right hip.

Mechanical complication — NOT a disease.
```

```
DANGER SIGNS — REPORT IMMEDIATELY
```

## SLIDE 35 — TAKE NOTE: FETAL MOVEMENT
**Image:** `1523_D2_S35_fetal-movement.png`
```
■ TAKE NOTE! FETAL MOVEMENT

Quickening: first felt 16–22 weeks.
Earlier in multiparas.

"Count to 10" method:
Lie left side, count movements daily.

If >2 hours to reach 10 movements
→ contact provider immediately.

Decreased movement = possible hypoxia.
```
